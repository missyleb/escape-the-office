⚠️ Obstacles (Avoid These!)

📅

Meeting Blocks

Ground obstacles - Jump over them! They represent endless meetings trying to trap you.

📄

Paperwork Stacks

Tall ground obstacles - Jump high to clear them! Bureaucracy at its finest.

💻

Calendar Notifications

Fall from above - Watch out! Sometimes you need to slide under them.

✨ Power-ups (Collect These!)

🎩

OOO Hat

Makes you invincible for 10 seconds! Pass through any obstacle while glowing gold.

☕

Coffee Boost

Speeds up the game for 5 seconds - more distance, more points, more chaos!

💎

Gamma Crystal

Instantly clears all obstacles on screen - your path to freedom!

🍌

Bonus Banana

Worth 500 points! Because why not?

🎯 Your Mission

You're a three-eyed cat trying to escape the corporate world! The office environment will try to stop you with meetings, paperwork, and calendar invites. Collect power-ups to help your escape. The longer you survive, the faster you'll need to run. Watch as the world transforms from gray office walls to beautiful freedom as you progress!
